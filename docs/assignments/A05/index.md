# A5 – Bracket

## Objective
Our task for this project was to design a bracket to support a rope carrying between 500 and 800 pounds of force via a strap connected to the bracket, with the bracket resting on a T-beam. From there, we take the bracket apart piece by piece to determine the geometry through calculations based on the material, which in my case I decided to use Titanium (Ti-6Al-V4), and the geometry we know. Using a safety factor of 4, we were given this diagram as the basis for our design.
