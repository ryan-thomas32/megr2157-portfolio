# A5 – Bracket

## Objective
Our task for this project was to design a bracket to support a rope carrying between 300 and 500 pounds via a strap connected to the bracket, with the bracket resting on a T-beam. From there, we take the bracket apart piece by piece to determine the geometry through calculations based on the material (ABS plastic) and the geometry we know. Using a safety factor of 4, we were given this diagram as the basis for our design.
